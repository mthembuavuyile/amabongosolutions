About Amabongo Solutions

Dedicated glass recycling specialists & industrial cullet suppliers operating in Pietermaritzburg, KwaZulu-Natal.

Our Mission & Core Focus

At Amabongo Solutions, we are dedicated exclusively to glass recycling and sustainable cullet supply. We bridge the gap between local bottle collectors, commercial businesses, municipal waste streams, and industrial glass manufacturers.

Immediate Cash Value

We buy unwashed and unsorted glass bottles, jars, and cullet direct from community collectors and businesses, offering instant cash payouts on site.

Industrial Cullet Supply

We supply color-sorted, crushed, high-purity glass cullet to container manufacturers, helping reduce furnace energy usage and carbon emissions.

Environmental Impact

By diverting thousands of tonnes of glass waste from South African landfills annually, we drive a cleaner, circular economy across KZN.

Facility Capabilities & Scale

Our operational hub in Mkhondeni is built for rapid vehicle turnaround, high-tonnage offloading, and continuous glass sorting.

2,507 m² Facility

Spacious industrial processing yard located at 72 CB Downes Road, Mkhondeni, Pietermaritzburg.

30 Tonnes/Day Capacity

Equipped to accept up to 30 tonnes of glass daily (currently processing 20 tonnes/day of cullet and whole bottles).

Heavy Equipment

Onsite forklifts and mechanical sorting/bailing equipment for efficient bulk container offloading.

Logistics Support

Client depot drop-offs welcomed, plus collections for PMB & surroundings (kgs to a few tonnes) and 34-tonne minimums for long-distance loads.

Regulatory Compliance & Governance

Amabongo Solutions is a registered enterprise (Reg: 2025/490106/07) adhering strictly to South African waste management and environmental standards.

SAPS Dealer Registered

Registered Second-Hand Goods Dealer with SAPS, guaranteeing full legal compliance for material buy-back operations.

DFFE Norms & Standards

Certified compliance for waste storage, sorting, grinding, crushing, screening, and bailing of glass streams.

Provincial Authorisation

Provincial Waste Management Authorisation held and active for all operational activities.

Digital Audit Trail

Weighbridge tickets and Safe Disposal Certificates issued via Recycle Pro-X management software.
