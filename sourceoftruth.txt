1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & COMPANY PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Amabongo Solutions is a South African glass recycling, buying, and cullet supply 
enterprise headquartered in Pietermaritzburg, KwaZulu-Natal. The business buys 
commercial and community glass waste (bottles and cullet), processes it, and supplies 
furnace-ready cullet to recycling partners and manufacturers.

Core Business Model:
- Purchases recyclable bottle glass from waste collectors, businesses, recycling buy-back 
  centres, venues, and individuals.
- Receives drop-offs directly at the Mkondeni depot in Pietermaritzburg.
- Dispatches collection trucks across KwaZulu-Natal and long-distance to other provinces 
  (Gauteng, Eastern Cape, etc.) and neighbouring territories for qualifying bulk loads.
- Aggregates, sorts by colour, and crushes bottle glass for industrial cullet reuse.

4. ACCEPTED VS. REJECTED MATERIALS (STRICT SPECIFICATIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Amabongo Solutions deals EXCLUSIVELY in container/bottle glass. 

ACCEPTED MATERIALS:
- Alcohol, beer, spirit, wine, and cider glass bottles.
- Soft drink and beverage glass containers.
- Food preserve and sauce glass jars.
- Whole, unbroken bottles.
- Unwashed bottles (dust, labels, and minor liquid residue are fully acceptable).
- Unsorted mixed-colour glass bottles.
- Sorted or crushed bottle glass by color:
  * Clear / White Flint Glass
  * Amber / Brown Glass
  * Green Glass
  * Mixed Bottle Cullet

STRICTLY REJECTED MATERIALS (DO NOT ACCEPT):
- Window pane glass and flat/architectural glass.
- Automotive glass / windscreens.
- Mirrors and mirrored glass.
- Light bulbs, fluorescent tubes, and globes.
- Heat-resistant glass, Pyrex, cookware, and ovenware.
- Ceramics, porcelain, clay, tiles, or pottery.
- Laminated, toughened, or wired safety glass.
- Cardboard, paper, or plastic (refer customers to local general recyclers such as 
  Mzimkhulu Scrap Metal).
- Metal scrap, cans, or batteries.


5. PRICING STRUCTURE & PAYOUT RATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Amabongo Solutions pays collectors based on verified scale weight upon receipt/collection.

Standard Purchase Rates (Depot Delivery / Standard Collection):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Material Category         | Rate per Kilogram | Rate per Metric Tonne
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Crushed Bottle Glass      | R0.60 / kg        | R600 / Tonne
Uncrushed Bottle Glass    | R0.50 / kg        | R500 / Tonne
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Bulk Volume Incentives (40 to 50+ Tonnes):
- For dedicated commercial bulk supplies exceeding 40 to 50 tonnes, higher rates 
  (e.g., R0.65/kg or R650/tonne for crushed glass) can be negotiated with management.

Distance / Logistics Deductions:
- When collecting sub-standard volumes or long-distance loads where transport is covered 
  by Amabongo Solutions, a discounted base rate (e.g. R0.45/kg for uncrushed) or a diesel 
  contribution may be applied upon review by the operations team.

Crushed vs. Uncrushed Economics:
- Crushing glass before packaging allows sellers to fit significantly more weight into 
  each bag, reduces required transport volume, and yields a higher payout (R600/t vs R500/t).
  Example calculation on 34 tonnes:
  * 34 tonnes Crushed:   34 × R600 = R20,400 payout
  * 34 tonnes Uncrushed: 34 × R500 = R17,000 payout
  * Net gain from crushing: +R3,400.

Payment Processing:
- Depot Drop-offs: Instant cash payout or instant EFT upon certified scale weighing.
- Truck Collections: Payout transferred directly into the seller's verified bank 
  account once the truck load is verified and weighed at the certified weighbridge.


6. COLLECTION POLICIES, LOGISTICS & MINIMUM LOAD THRESHOLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Due to high truck operating costs and diesel expenses, Amabongo Solutions applies strict 
tiered collection rules based on travel time and distance from Pietermaritzburg.

Tier 1: Customer Self-Delivery to Depot (Mkondeni, PMB)
- Minimum Volume: NO MINIMUM.
- Walk-ins, bakkie loads, trailers, and independent trucks can deliver any quantity 
  (from a few kilograms up to multiple tonnes).

Tier 2: Local & Regional Collections (Within PMB & Surrounds, < 1 to 2 Hours Drive)
- Examples: Pietermaritzburg, Midlands, Mafakatini, Howick, Hilton, Camperdown.
- Minimum Volume: Flexible commercial batches (e.g., 20 one-tonne bags / 20 tonnes agreed 
  on case-by-case evaluation).

Tier 3: Long-Distance & Out-of-Province Collections (2+ Hours Drive / Outside KZN)
- Examples: Johannesburg/Gauteng (approx. 5.5 hours), Jozini (approx. 5 hours), 
  Eastern Cape, Swaziland/Eswatini border routes.
- STRICT MINIMUM: 34 Metric Tonnes (34 one-tonne bags) — one full commercial truckload.
- Loads below 34 tonnes are NOT commercially viable for Amabongo Solutions trucks to collect 
  over long distances.


7. PRE-COLLECTION PROTOCOL & WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before any collection truck is booked or dispatched, the seller must complete this 
4-step verification:

Step 1: Exact Location Pin & Site Classification
- The seller must provide an exact Google Maps location pin, city, and suburb.
- The seller must clarify site conditions:
  * Dump / Landfill site
  * Township
  * Rural area
  * Commercial / Industrial premises / Venue

Step 2: Packaging in 1-Tonne Bulk Bags
- All glass (crushed or uncrushed) must be packed into heavy-duty 1-tonne bulk bags 
  (jumbo bags). Loose, unpackaged piles will not be collected by long-haul trucks.

Step 3: Photographic / Video Evidence
- The seller must send photos or videos via WhatsApp showing the fully packed, ready bags 
  on site to verify the volume and material quality before dispatch.

Step 4: Dispatch & Loading
- Once verified by the office, collection is scheduled (usually same-day or next business day 
  subject to truck availability).
- Trucks do NOT travel with forklifts. The seller is responsible for providing sufficient 
  manual labour (e.g., 3-4 workers) to assist in loading the bags onto the truck bed.


8. EQUIPMENT, BAGS & PACKAGING GUIDANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Supply of Bags:
- Amabongo Solutions DOES NOT sell, supply, or provide 1-tonne bulk bags to collectors.
- Sellers must source their own bulk bags (1-tonne woven polypropylene jumbo bags) 
  from local industrial packaging suppliers or online retailers.

Crushing Equipment:
- Amabongo Solutions does not provide crushing machinery to collectors.
- Commercial small crushing machines range from R13,000 to R40,000+.
- For collectors starting out without machines, Amabongo Solutions recommends a safe, 
  controlled manual crushing method inside heavy-duty drums/containers with full personal 
  protective equipment (safety goggles, cut-resistant gloves, and heavy boots).

10. INTERNATIONAL INQUIRIES & EXPORT POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Policy on Export Requests (e.g., Angola, Mozambique, SADC):
- Core Focus: 100% focused on the South African domestic recycling market.
- Customs & Ocean Freight: Amabongo Solutions does not handle SARS export licensing, 
  customs clearing, container sea freight (FOB/CIF), port handling, or international shipping.
- Export Inquiries: If an international buyer wishes to purchase cullet (up to 80 tonnes/month 
  of clear flint), the buyer must arrange and pay for all freight, shipping, customs, 
  and export logistics from Durban harbour, or collect locally within South Africa.
- Indicative baseline price for clear flint cullet prepared for export pickup: 
  R2.00/kg (R2,000/tonne ≈ USD $124/tonne).
