1. Introduction & Overview

AMABONGO SOLUTIONS t/a AMABONGO GLASS RECYCLING ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through our official website (www.amabongosolutions.co.za).

This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal information in compliance with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (Act 4 of 2013), commonly known as POPIA.

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when submitting inquiries or trade requests:

  • Contact & Trade Enquiries: Full Name, Company/Business Name, Email Address, Phone/WhatsApp Number, City/Suburb Location, and details of your glass waste or cullet supply requirements.
  • Communication Data: Any information contained within messages or correspondence sent directly to us via WhatsApp, email, or telephone.

3. How Information is Processed & Transmitted

We believe in full transparency regarding how our website functions:

  • Direct WhatsApp & Email Routing: When you complete and submit our online trade enquiry form on contact.html, your entries are formatted into a direct message and sent straight to our official WhatsApp account (+27 64 878 4287) or email address. Our website does not store or maintain your form entries in a server-side database.
  • Client-Side Virtual Assistant (Chatbot): Our website features an interactive Virtual Assistant powered entirely by lightweight, local JavaScript running in your web browser. The chatbot does not record, save, store, or transmit your conversation inputs or personal data to external server databases or third-party AI services.

We process personal information solely for legitimate commercial purposes, including:

  • Responding to quote requests (RFQs) for glass buying, cullet supply, or bulk recycling services.
  • Scheduling commercial glass collection services for businesses, hospitality venues, or industrial partners.
  • Verifying vendor onboarding and supplier registrations.
  • Fulfilling customer service inquiries and logistical arrangements at our Mkondeni depot.
  • Complying with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations under South African law.

5. Storage, Security & Retention

Personal information transmitted to us via WhatsApp, email, or telephone is handled securely by authorized personnel at AMABONGO SOLUTIONS. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to comply with statutory legal record-keeping requirements.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Personal information may only be shared under the following limited circumstances:

  • With authorized service providers or logistics partners directly assisting in fulfilling your recycling collections or deliveries.
  • When required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order issued by South African authorities.

8. Your Rights Under POPIA

Under Section 5 of POPIA, data subjects have specific rights regarding their personal information. You have the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you and receive a copy of that information.
  • Correction: Request the correction, updating, or destruction of personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, out of date, or incomplete.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your personal information on reasonable grounds.
  • Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with the South African Information Regulator if you believe your rights under POPIA have been infringed.
