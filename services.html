Commercial Glass Recycling

Bulk collections & B2B recycling services across KwaZulu-Natal. We buy unwashed & unsorted glass for instant cash.

Two Ways to Recycle With Us

Whether you're a household dropping off a bag of bottles or a business generating tonnes of glass monthly — we turn waste glass into instant cash.

Depot Drop-Off

Bring your glass directly to our Mkondeni facility. We buy unwashed & unsorted, crushed or uncrushed glass for self-delivery. No appointment needed — just arrive, we weigh, and we pay cash instantly.

  • We buy unwashed & unsorted
  • Crushed or uncrushed glass
  • Instant cash payment
  • Open Mon–Sat

Commercial Collection

We dispatch our vehicles to collect from your premises. Truck collections require a minimum of 34 tonnes (we highly recommend crushing glass for these loads to maximize capacity and payout). For smaller loads, we collect bagged glass with bakkies depending on location.

  • Large truck pickups (34t min)
  • Bakkie collections for smaller loads
  • Crushing recommended for bulk
  • Serving greater KZN Midlands

Who We Collect From

Any business generating glass waste qualifies for our commercial collection service. The more consistent your volume, the better the arrangement we can offer.

Restaurants & Hotels

Regular weekly or bi-weekly pickups for food service businesses and guest houses generating bottle and jar waste, perfectly aligned to your housekeeping cycle.

Bottle Stores

High-volume amber and green glass generators. We handle large loads efficiently directly from your premises.

Food & Bev Manufacturers

Contracted bulk collection for production waste, cullet, and off-spec glass from manufacturing and bottling lines.

Distributors & Offices

We handle breakage, slow-moving glass stock, and service centralised glass collection points for commercial office parks.
